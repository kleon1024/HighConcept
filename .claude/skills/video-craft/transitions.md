# Transitions (转场)

The user's complaint: **"每一幕都没啥关系"** — scenes felt unrelated. A generic colour wipe between unrelated scenes is
a cut with decoration. Every transition must **hand something over** from scene A to scene B. Plan the hand-off in the
storyboard (a "carry" column: what crosses the cut).

The toolkit (prefer the top of the list):
1. **Match cut on shape** — the circle of the arena becomes the pupil becomes the moon becomes the cell. Same position,
   same size, new meaning.
2. **Morph** — the object itself transforms into the next scene's object (a neuron's spike becomes the blue pulse on
   the scope; the alga's eyespot unfolds into the membrane).
3. **Zoom-through** — push into a detail until it fills the frame and becomes the next world (into the eyespot → the
   channel; into the DOM → the ice; through a ring gate → the next realm).
4. **Carry-over element** — one element stays on screen across the cut and is re-used (the fibre, the light beam, a
   caption that rewrites itself, a particle that keeps flying).
5. **Motion continuity** — exit direction = entry direction; a whip-pan out of A lands as a whip-pan into B; a falling
   object in A lands in B.
6. **Colour bridge** — the last dominant colour of A is the background of B (blue flash → blue title).
7. **Light / graphic wipe from the world** — the beam of light, a wave of ions, a sweeping crack, an ink stroke wipes
   the frame; never an arbitrary slab.
8. **Sound bridge (J/L-cut)** — B's sound starts 0.2–0.5 s before B's picture, or A's tail carries into B.
9. **Narrative bridge** — A ends on a question, B opens on the answer (see narrative.md).

Craft rules:
- Cut on the beat (bars of 2 s at 120 BPM in our scores); the whoosh peaks on the cut frame.
- Keep transitions short (0.3–0.6 s) unless the transition *is* the shot (a zoom-through can be 1–2 s).
- Vary the toolkit through a film, but keep one signature transition that recurs (e.g. always zoom-through for scale changes).
- Avoid: identical diagonal wipes everywhere, crossfades between unrelated graphics, hard cuts with no carry.
