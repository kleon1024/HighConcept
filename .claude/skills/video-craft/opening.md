# Opening (开场)

The first 1–3 seconds decide whether anyone stays. Open on **suspense, a question, a counter-intuitive fact or an impact**.

Patterns that work:
- **The question**: a short, provocative question over a striking image — 「一束光，能遥控大脑吗？」 Then answer fast.
- **The anomaly**: show the strangest true thing first — 「南极冰下两公里，埋着 5160 只“眼睛”」.
- **The montage of payoffs**: three rapid proofs, each on a hit — 「叫醒它 / 吓住它 / 让它转圈」 — then reveal the cause.
- **The twist on the viewer**: make it about them — 「而此刻，它正穿过你。」
- **The impact**: a real switch, a flash, a slam of type on frame 1 — with sound from frame 1.

Rules:
- No logo, no title card, no "今天我们来聊…" before the hook. The title can come at 8–12 s, once the viewer is hooked.
- Real imagery (a real place, real lab, real object) in the opening buys credibility; animation explains after.
- Escalate: question → proof → bigger proof → reveal. Each beat 0.8–1.5 s, every beat on the music grid.
- Sound must be present and characterful from frame 1 (a low hit, a switch, a breath) — a silent opening reads as broken.
- Text in the opening: ≤ 10 characters per line, huge, centred in the upper third.

Feedback log: 「开头冲击要大」「视频开头要更有吸引力，最好以悬念，问题，反常识，冲击力开场」「光遗传的开头不够吸引人」.
