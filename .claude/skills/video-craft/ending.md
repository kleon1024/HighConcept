# Ending (结尾升华)

Don't end on a dry fact. In the last 4–6 seconds, **lift**:
- **Bookend**: return to the opening image or question, now understood differently.
- **Widen the frame**: from the detail to the human or cosmic scale (from a pond cell to a beam of light into the brain;
  from one neutrino to a new kind of astronomy).
- **One resonant line**: short, quotable, emotional or philosophical — 「一切，始于一个看似“没用”的问题」.
- **Visual and musical crescendo, then a breath**: the biggest chord lands with the line, then hold 1–1.5 s of calm.
- Keep credits/sources small and last; never let them cover the line.
- **A curtain call (谢幕)** can carry the credits with grace: the paintings or characters return one by one under a
  spotlight, labelled with their role and their maker, while the theme plays once more (and, for a staged film, the
  audience applauds); then the curtain closes on the title.
