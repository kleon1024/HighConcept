# 道 · 空 — Laozi and Zhuangzi on emptiness, 78 seconds

## The idea
**命题：「空，才有用。」** (on screen marked 老子 · 大意: Laozi says 无, not 空, so the real line, 「无之以为用」, is shown in the title.) Daoism is often summed up as "doing nothing". This film picks one claim a viewer can see
and test. In 《道德经》 chapter 11 the useful part of a wheel, a bowl or a room is the empty part. The film then
follows that idea through Chinese painting (留白), water, Zhuangzi's cook, Zhuangzi's useless tree, taiji and the
butterfly. It ends on a modern life with every square filled, and the advice to leave some white.

Style: a Song painting's emptiness. The film uses raw paper, black ink, one cinnabar seal and real paintings, with
nothing drawn that isn't ink. A brush (`Brush` in `kit.tsx`) presses down round, runs full through the middle and lifts
into dry streaks (飞白). One empty ink circle runs through the film:
- the hub of a wheel
- the hollow of a bowl
- a moon gate
- the taiji
- the moon over Ma Yuan's cold river

Music: guqin, 埙 (xun), 箫, water and wind. The theme has rests written into it (D — B A — G), and at 「那片白」 the
whole score stops for a breath. Dark and soft: every stem is low-passed and the master has no presence boost.
