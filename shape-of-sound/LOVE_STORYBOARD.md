# 什么是爱 — a play in five acts, 98 seconds

(`src/love/`). Timing and cues: `src/love/love.json` (shared with the score). Score and sound design: `music/love.py`
(cue sheet in `out/love_cues.md`). Paintings: `media/media.json` (`lv_*`), credits in `media/MEDIA_CREDITS.md`.

## The idea
The film is staged as a play. A red velvet curtain with gold embroidery opens on the question, falls at the
intermission between joy and grief, and closes at the end. Between those moments, every act takes the art style of its
painting. Joy and sorrow alternate, and the film ends with a curtain call in which the paintings take their bow.

The spine is *The Little Prince*. Its answer is that love means **taming**, which the fox explains as *créer des liens*,
creating ties. Love is also the time you "waste" on a rose, and staying responsible for what you have tamed. The other
works add what that answer leaves out: longing (Plato), giving (Shakespeare), loss (元好问, 归有光), maturity (Rilke,
Fromm), and a cascade of classic lines (汤显祖, 《哥林多前书》, 苏轼). Klimt's gold is the climax. The film ends by asking
the viewer one question and giving one thing to do.

Through-line: one **gold thread**, the fox's 「建立联系」. It comes out of the rose that lights up in the garden, draws
Plato's circle, runs from Romeo to his line, ties the one dot to the fox, winds round the clock, stretches between
the prince and the fox as they part, rings the rose, grows into the loquat tree, is woven into cloth (Fromm), becomes
Klimt's gold, and at the end runs out of the frame towards the viewer.

Music: one waltz theme, the "four o'clock waltz" (F major, 3/4), re-orchestrated in every act. See the score's header for
the full plan: overture; lyre and aulos; romantic strings; a storybook waltz that stops dead at the 5,000 roses; a Paris
musette; a ticking accelerando; full orchestra for the wheat; a solo cello; solo piano; 箫 and 古琴; a Baroque chorale;
a minimalist ostinato; Mahler-style Vienna for Klimt; and for the curtain call the whole orchestra plus applause.
