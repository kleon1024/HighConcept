## The idea
**命题：「一个人，成不了“仁”」.** 《说文》 reads 仁 as 人 + 二 (从人从二). The film opens on a lone 亻 carved in
black stone. Two strokes slide in beside it, and those **two strokes of 二 carry the whole film** (`src/ru/motif.tsx`):

1. the rods of the first painting
2. the two strokes between two 人
3. the ruled lines of a stele, then a balance beam between 己 and 人
4. the stone (己) dropped into water, whose rings take over
5. the rods of the family painting
6. the road of the fourteen-year journey
7. a gate's lintel and sill
8. the two lacquered beams of the 曾侯乙 bell rack
9. the glyph again, then the rods of the portrait at the curtain call

The film moves outward like 费孝通's ripples: one heart, two people, the family, the road, the school, the world.
It ends on 「德不孤，必有邻」 and 「你从来不是一个人」, a bookend to the hook's 「一个人，做不到」.

**Style: 碑拓 (stele rubbing).** The ground is black ink pounded over paper on stone, with the characters left
white. The type is 楷书 (LXGW WenKai TC) through a "rubbed" filter that mottles the white the way a rubbing does.
Cinnabar is used only for the word that matters. Every image is real:

- the Southern Song 《孝经图》
- Li Gonglin's 《孝经图》
- a Western Zhou 簋
- the 吴道子 Confucius stele rubbing
- the 曾侯乙 bells
- Ma Yuan's portrait for the curtain call

**Music: 金声玉振**, from Mencius on Confucius as 集大成, after the 释奠 rite in which each phrase opens on bells and
closes on stone. The theme is four notes, like a four-character hymn line:

- The 埙 plays it alone and stops short of the last note.
- The 琴 answers with the missing note: two voices finish one phrase, the way 二 completes 人.
- The 八音 gather as the rings widen, and the rhythm goes from 1 event a bar to 2, then 4, then back to 1.
- The bell's two-tone third turns from minor to major on the last line (the 曾侯乙 bells really do sound two notes a
  third apart).
- 柷 opens and 敔 closes. There are no bright bells.
