# 意义，从哪来？— 60 秒，15 种回答

16:9 · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · **60 s** (120 BPM) · Remotion composition `Meaning` (`src/meaning/`)
Timing and cues: `src/meaning/meaning.json` (shared with the score). Score and sound design: `music/meaning.py` (cue sheet
in `out/meaning_cues.md`). Artworks: `media/media.json` (`me_*`), credits in `media/MEDIA_CREDITS.md`.

## Why a redo
The lamp version was judged rough: hand-drawn figures looked deformed, one flat style throughout, too few ideas. This
version draws no people at all: **every era is shown through a real public-domain masterpiece of its own art movement**,
overlaid with bold geometry, and every era has its own music and its own sounds. One question ties fifteen schools.

## The idea
**意义，从哪来？** Each school gives one answer, shown at the top of its segment as 「意义 + 在……」: in the sky (Plato), in
nature (Diogenes), in what you control (the Stoics), in God (Augustine), in the human (Renaissance), in "I think"
(Descartes), in morality (Kant vs Bentham), in the blind will (Schopenhauer), in changing the world (Marx), "God is dead,
so make it yourself" (Nietzsche), in your choices (Sartre, Camus), in how words are used (Wittgenstein), in the gaze of
power (Foucault), everywhere and nowhere (postmodernism), "we've seen through it all" (modern cynicism). The ending:
意义，不是找到的——是你活出来的。

Through-line: **a circle** (meaning), drawn by a compass in frame 1 and redrawn by every era in its own style — Plato's
ideal circle and dodecahedron, the sun Diogenes asks Alexander not to block (an eclipse), the Stoic circle of control in
mosaic tiles, the Gothic rose window, Leonardo's circle and square, the Cartesian origin, Schopenhauer's pendulum,
Lissitzky's white circle pierced by the red wedge, Nietzsche's sun shattering, Sisyphus' stone, the Malevich square, the
Panopticon's rings, pop-art dots — and in the end an open, hand-drawn circle left for the viewer.

Every segment carries a trio card — **哲学 / 艺术 / 声音** — so the viewer learns the art movement and the music of each
era at the same time.
