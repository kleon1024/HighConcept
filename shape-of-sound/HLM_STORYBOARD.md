## The idea
**命题：「第一页就写好了所有人的结局」.** In its first chapters the novel tells you, in plain sight, how everyone
will end: in the leftover stone, in the names, in the plays at the happiest party, in the dream register. 脂砚斋, who
knew the author and the lost ending, decoded much of it in red ink in the margins. The film reads the book that way.

**Style.** The film has three layers:

- **The wall.** A white garden wall with a plum-blossom window (梅花窗, modelled on one in 扬州何园). Every picture
  is seen through it, and the first cut zooms through it.
- **The pictures.** 改琦's woodblock portraits from 《红楼梦图咏》, 孙温's 大观园 and 费丹旭's 黛玉葬花.
- **The page.** A manuscript page with red column rules. The novel's text is set in black type (Noto Serif SC, like
  a printed edition), and every decoding is brushed beside it in red (Zhi Mang Xing), like a real 朱批.

Every claim carries a small label saying where it comes from: 正文 (the text) or 脂批 (the commentary). Where readers
joined the commentary into a phrase the 批 never wrote, the label says so.

The colour drains with the story: warm paper in the garden, grey at the dream, white at 「白茫茫大地真干净」, then
warm paper again for the first page.

**Music (Kunqu colours, original melodies; nothing from the 1987 TV score, no recordings).** The palette is 曲笛, 笙,
檀板, 单皮鼓, 三弦, 琵琶, 筝 and 箫; the novel itself stages its ch. 5 songs with 「檀板」 and 「银筝」.

- **The motif.** The four sisters' names, 元迎探惜 = 原应叹息, become four notes of 南曲 pentatonic: D, E, F♯, A.
  The motif always stops after three notes. On 惜/息 the music gives nothing but the clapper. Only on the last line
  does 息 (A) sound and fall home on a breath; 息 means both "sigh" and "rest".
- **The beat stretches as the family falls (板式递减).** It runs 流水板 and 一板一眼 in the garden, 一板三眼 at the dream,
  赠板 for 葬花, then 散板 (free time) in the white.
- **好了歌** is set as a beggar's 数板: dry 三弦 and clapper, with every 「好」 a fourth above every 「了」. That falling
  fourth ends each sister's fate.
